About the Culture

Philippine culture is a vibrant fusion of indigenous Austronesian traditions and centuries of Spanish, American,
and Asian influences, anchored by close-knit family ties, deep religious faith, and an enduring spirit of community resilience.
Social life revolves around respect for elders, warm hospitality, and bayanihan—the tradition of neighbors coming together to help one another.
While Roman Catholicism shapes the nation's colorful town fiestas and holidays, a rich Islamic heritage in the south and diverse indigenous customs add depth across the islands.
Expressed through a passionate love for music, folk dances, over 170 regional languages,
and a flavorful cuisine that blends sweet, sour, and salty notes, Filipino culture celebrates joy, togetherness, and adaptability.

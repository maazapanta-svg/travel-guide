The Philippines is an archipelagic nation in Southeast Asia situated in the western Pacific Ocean. Geographic Location
• Region: Southeast Asia, as part of the Malay Archipelago.
• Coordinates: Roughly between 4°23′–21°25′ N latitude and 116°–127° E longitude.
• Surrounding Bodies of Water:
• East: Philippine Sea / Pacific Ocean
• West and Northwest: South China
• South: Celebes Sea
• Southwest: Sulu Sea
• North: Bashi Channel (separating it from Taiwan)
• Neighbors: Taiwan to the north, Borneo/Malaysia to the southwest, and Indonesia to the south.

Featured Destinations

Palawan

Palawan is an archipelagic province in the western Philippines located in the MIMAROPA region,
situated between the West Philippine Sea and the Sulu Sea.
Known globally as the country's "Last Frontier,"
it spans over 1,700 islands and islets famed for dramatic limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, and rich biodiversity.
General Information
• Capital and Access. The provincial capital is Puerto Princesa, which serves as the main transport hub
alongside regional airports in El Nido (Lio Airport), Coron (Busuanga Airport), and San Vicente. • Climate and Best Time to Visit. Palawan has a tropical climate with average temperatures between 24°C and 29°C.
The dry season from late November to May offers the calmest seas and clearest skies, while June to October brings monsoon rains. • Language and Currency. The official currency is the Philippine Peso (PHP), and
English is widely spoken alongside Filipino and local dialects like Cuyonon Famous Attractions
Palawan's top sights stretch from its northern Calamian Islands down to its central and southern coastlines:
Puerto Princesa El Nido
Why should foreigners visit?
The Philippines offers a rare mix of world-class beaches, rich biodiversity, warm hospitality, and affordable luxury. Because the country spans over 7,600 islands, travelers can experience vastly different landscapes and cultures in a single trip.

Boracay


• Location and Info. Located off the northwest tip of Panay Island in Aklan (Western Visayas), accessible via Caticlan Airport and a short boat transfer.
This compact 7-kilometer island is famous for its powdery white sand and vibrant beachside dining. • Famous Attractions. White Beach for calm swimming and sunsets, Puka Shell Beach for quieter shores,
Ariel's Point for cliff diving, and Mount Luho for panoramic island views. • Why Foreigners Should Visit.
It offers world-class beachfront resorts, reliable infrastructure, and lively
nightlife alongside strict environmental rules that keep the water crystal clear.

Bohol


• Location and Info. Situated in the Central Visayas region southeast of Cebu, reached via Panglao International Airport
or a two-hour fast ferry from Cebu City. It blends dramatic inland geology with white-sand beach resorts on Panglao Island. • Famous Attractions. The Chocolate Hills (over 1,200 symmetrical cone-shaped mounds),
the Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary in Corella, Loboc River floating restaurant cruises, and Alona Beach on Panglao. • Why Foreigners Should Visit. Bohol delivers an easy, family-friendly mix of rare wildlife,
unique landscapes, heritage churches, and world-class diving at nearby Balicasag Island.

Siargo

• Location and Info.
A teardrop-shaped island in Surigao del Norte off the northeastern coast of Mindanao,
accessible via direct flights to Sayak Airport. Known as the Surfing Capital of the Philippines,
it maintains a laid-back, barefoot island atmosphere. •
Famous Attractions: Cloud 9 surf break and wooden boardwalk, Sugba Lagoon for paddleboarding, Magpupungko
Rock Pools, and island hopping to Naked, Daku, and Guyam Islands. •
Why Foreigners Should Visit. It attracts travelers seeking surf culture, lush coconut palm landscapes,
boutique eco-stays, and a social, creative international community.
